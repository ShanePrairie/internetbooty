THE USEFUL PART

What you should know.

Do I need special software?

No. The hunt is designed around the web. A normal modern browser and your own puzzle-solving tools are enough to start.

Is the world chat part of the hunt?

No. It is a social room for paid Crew members. It exists for community and atmosphere, not as a source of privileged hunt clues.

Can I join after launch?

The public path is planned to open with the hunt. Early Crew is the pre-launch path and carries the lower entry price while it is available.

Why is so much still vague?

Because discovery is part of the fun. You should understand what you're entering without having the experience explained to death before it begins.
